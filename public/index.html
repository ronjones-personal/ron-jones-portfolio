How I lead

My job as a leader is to block and tackle: remove what's in the team's way, put processes in place that make the team stronger and more proactive, and keep iterating toward excellence.

Remove blockers
I clear the path across Legal, Finance, Product, and Delivery, so deals and projects don't stall waiting on another team.
Build processes that make the team proactive
Qualification standards, deal reviews, and an operating cadence that let the team see problems coming instead of reacting to them.
Iterate toward excellence
Launch the first version of a process, measure it, and keep improving it. Good systems get built over time, not announced.
Develop leaders
Hiring, onboarding and ramp design, career paths, and coaching senior individual contributors into managers.

AI is a force multiplier when it's built into how the team works

I use it three ways: to create go-to-market systems, to keep them healthy, and to see what's happening in the pipeline sooner.

Creating systems
Playbooks, discovery frameworks, demo narratives, workflow automations, and quick prototypes of internal tools. The team gets a working version to react to sooner, and I can build the first draft myself.
Maintaining systems
CRM data quality and revenue data governance, documentation that stays current, and routine workflow steps that run on their own, so people spend their time with customers instead of on upkeep.
Getting insights
AI-assisted forecasting and deal intelligence: spotting risk in the pipeline, summarizing accounts and calls, and briefing the team before discovery, competitive, and executive meetings.

AI belongs in the way of work, with clear standards for how the team uses it, not off to the side as a pilot.At Coherent I led the agentic AI offering from development through launch and built its go-to-market motion AI-native from the start.
